## 🌾 About the Project

SoilSathi is an initiative to help farmers understand the health of their farms through simple insights and actionable recommendations.

It focuses on soil health, crop conditions, and sustainable farming practices to help farmers make informed decisions while protecting soil fertility for future generations.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🌱 **Soil Health Analysis** – Understand soil conditions and nutrient requirements.
- 🌿 **Crop Health Monitoring** – Identify potential crop health issues.
- 💡 **Smart Recommendations** – Get practical suggestions for better farming.
- 💧 **Water Conservation** – Encourage responsible water usage and irrigation.
- ♻️ **Soil Sustainability** – Promote balanced nutrient management and long-term soil fertility.
- 📱 **Offline Accessibility** – Access saved reports and essential information in low-connectivity areas.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make sustainable agriculture easier and more accessible by combining technology, AI, and practical farming insights.

> Don't just grow better crops. Build healthier soil for generations to come.
